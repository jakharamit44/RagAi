🏛 University & Enterprise Portal Integration

This demo page illustrates how the RagAi Chat Widget embeds directly into any student portal or employee intranet.

Student Portal Integration

Configured with data-role="student" and data-department="Computer Science".

Enables queries regarding semester examination schemes, course syllabi, laboratory manuals, and lecture notes.

Employee / Staff Intranet Integration

Configured with data-role="employee" and data-department="Human Resources".

Enables staff queries on leave balances, pension circulars, medical reimbursement claims, and statutory policies.

👉 Notice the floating blue circular chat bubble in the bottom right corner of this screen. Click it to open the live RagAi Copilot!
